You may be here because

The fight has a script, and both of you know your lines.

  • One pursues while the other withdraws.
  • One manages while the other resists.
  • One person feels alone. The other feels controlled.
  • You repair the argument but not the pattern.
  • You both want closeness, but the loop gets there first.

How RelationSync helps

We stop asking who started it and start mapping what keeps producing it.

Map the loop

What happens before the first sharp sentence?

Name the roles

Who manages, who adapts, who shuts down, who carries the risk?

Practice repair

We build a different response before the old loop takes the wheel.
