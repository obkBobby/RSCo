Keep it brief

A few details are enough to start.

  • Your name
  • Your preferred path: individual, couples, or group coaching (or say you are unsure)
  • Your time zone
  • A brief goal: what would you like to do differently?

Please do not send clinical history, diagnoses, records, or sensitive details about yourself or your partner. This is an email inquiry, not a clinical intake. Email is not a crisis-support channel.

What happens next

Approval comes before payment or scheduling.

1. Robert reviews your inquiry

You discuss fit, what you want to work on, and timing. Group cohort dates have not been announced. An inquiry is not automatic approval or enrollment.

2. Sign the agreement

If Robert approves the fit and you choose to proceed, you receive and sign the coaching agreement before payment.

3. Confirm payment

After the signed agreement, Robert provides the Stripe payment step. Payment must be confirmed before scheduling.

4. Arrange Zoom sessions

Robert manually schedules your Zoom sessions with you after payment is confirmed. This page does not book an appointment.

Scheduling

Check timing with Robert.

Coaching availability windows are Tuesday, 2 to 4 p.m., and Thursday, noon to 3 p.m. and 7 to 9 p.m., in America/New_York (Eastern Time).

These are availability windows, not guaranteed openings or confirmed group dates. Robert will confirm a mutually workable schedule with you.

Coaching and education are not therapy, medical care, or crisis support. If there is abuse, coercion, violence, or immediate danger, prioritize safety and professional support.

Program terms

Payment and scheduling

Payment is due in full. Robert does not offer installment plans.

Individual and couples programs include 8 private sessions over 16 weeks. You may reschedule up to 2 private sessions with at least 24 hours notice. Each makeup must happen within 14 days of its original date, and all sessions must be completed within 18 weeks of the program start. Late cancellations and no-shows count as used sessions. The final 2 weeks are a maximum makeup buffer, not additional coaching.

Group coaching follows 8 fixed cohort dates over 16 weeks. Please give at least 24 hours notice if you cannot attend. Group absences do not include private makeup sessions or an extended program. Teaching recordings are available; personal hot seats are not recorded by default.

Payments are nonrefundable for a change of mind, withdrawal, missed sessions, or unused sessions. Mandatory legal rights still apply. If Robert cancels a session, it does not use your reschedule allowance. If he cannot provide a replacement within the program deadline, you receive a refund for the undelivered service.

Coaching takes place in scheduled sessions. Email outside sessions is for inquiries and administrative scheduling only; private message, text, and voice coaching are not included.
